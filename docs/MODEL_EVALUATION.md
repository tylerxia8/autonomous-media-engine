# Model and experiment evaluation

Use immutable as-of features and labels by platform and age. Proposed primary view labels are ≥1M and ≥2M within seven days. Record longer horizons separately. Include unsuccessful and low-view published posts. Track rejected and unpublished assignments for intent-to-treat value, with their view label unobserved. Keep pending and suppressed metrics censored. Never code them as zero views. Observe cash on a separate settlement window including reversals.

Train on earlier cohorts, calibrate on later cohorts and test once on a newer untouched block. Apply an embargo spanning feature/label overlap. Also hold out unseen creators and group near-identical assets/episodes so variants cannot leak across partitions. Baselines are heuristic ranking, age/channel baseline and regularized logistic regression. Compare content-only, trend and multimodal ablations.

Report Brier score, log loss, reliability by bin, precision-recall AUC and lift/precision at render budget K. Report metrics and intervals for both thresholds by platform and cohort. Resample by creator/campaign where feasible. Pre-register sample size from event rate and minimum meaningful lift before opening test data. If rare outcomes are too sparse, collect evidence and keep heuristic decisions. Promote only with better out-of-sample calibration and rank lift plus positive incremental realized net cash under a preregistered, mature randomized pilot.

Block randomization by creator/campaign/time as practical. Use one assignment per source unit, log propensity and treatment before exposure, then measure intent-to-treat net cash per assignment including rejected and costly failures. No duplicate public variants for testing. Report withdrawal, nulls and accounting lag. Stop on guardrail breach. A contextual bandit needs bounded exploration, reliable propensities, off-policy checks with overlap, a randomized comparator, and a rollback. Measure inference and owner time as additional costs.
