# Viral Intelligence Engine

Generate spans at sentence and silence boundaries. Enforce length, intelligibility and context. Rank deterministically using versioned features for first-seconds clarity, self-contained payoff, useful or surprising material, speech quality and relevance. Penalize unresolved pronouns, misleading boundaries, repetition and dead air. Prefer distinct moments and log why low-value spans were skipped. Pilot weights are heuristic and have no calibrated probability interpretation.

Process transcript and cheap envelope/silence statistics first. Sample scene changes or sparse frames only for shortlisted spans. Run higher-cost extraction only when its upper cost is reserved and forecast value justifies it. Persist measurement provenance, missingness, cutoff, config and score explanations. Never rely on captions alone for consent, correctness or disclosure.

Track permitted public metadata for time-aligned peers by platform, language, niche and account scale. On comparable post ages, calculate log view velocity and median/MAD deviation. Proposed floor 20 distinct peer posts and threshold robust z ≥3.5. Suppress zero-dispersion, small or stale cohorts. Flag outliers for hypothesis generation without copying assets or conflating competitor reach with AAME payout.

Snapshot approved trends at decision time by topic, geography, time and origin. Missing signals stay missing and use the baseline. Retention experiments vary truthful hooks, crop and pacing without distorting context. Track first measurable drop-off, average percent viewed and completion where supported. Require mature profit evidence for strategic promotion. See [evaluation](MODEL_EVALUATION.md).
