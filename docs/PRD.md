# Product requirements

Draft baseline dated 2026-11-26. The standalone V2.0 PRD was unavailable. Reconcile its clauses against the [backlog](REQUIREMENTS.md) before specification sign-off.

## Outcomes

1. Clear the first legitimate payout and reach cumulative verified cash break-even as quickly as possible.
2. Automate permitted steps and reduce steady-state owner time below 15 minutes per week after setup.
3. Reach $10/day operating profit as an initial target. Report trailing seven-day realized profit per day and recognize settlement lumpiness.
4. Improve the rate of 1M and ≥2M view clips per mature published cohort through measured experiments. Compare like platforms and label windows and report denominators and uncertainty.
5. Maximize realized profit, accounting for direct costs, failures, fees, settlement time and reversals.

## Scope and milestones

P0 aims for a seven-day capable revenue pipeline where eligibility, permissions and payer turnaround permit. Discover and qualify a campaign or direct paid pilot, authorize ingestion, score moments, edit and caption, independently check policy and media, publish, submit and reconcile. Use a cleared manual handoff when an official automation path is unavailable. Proof of actual cash is a separate milestone from completing a pipeline or receiving approval.

P1 adds research-based ranking, trend and competitor snapshots, outlier alerts, historical outcomes and experiments. P2 adds calibrated rare-event prediction, then contextual bandits and advanced multimodal inference only after positive measured economics. Authorized livestream clipping has no role on the first-cash critical path.

## Hard constraints

Maximum initial capital at risk $50, first-week spend $30, monthly ceiling $300. Require verified cash break-even and owner authorization to unlock the full monthly ceiling. Never raise an effective limit without owner authorization. Keep estimates, approvals, cash and expenses separate. Only process content with affirmative rights and only use official APIs or explicitly permitted workflows. Reject artificial engagement, duplicate spam and misleading edits. Default dry-run, isolate dev from production, treat external inputs as untrusted. Setup authorizes repository documentation only. See [requirements](REQUIREMENTS.md) and [financial policy](FINANCIAL_MODEL.md).
